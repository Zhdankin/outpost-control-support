Outpost Control Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 11, 2026

Data collection

Outpost Control does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, accounts, or third-party software development kits.

Data stored on your device

Game progress and sound and haptic preferences are stored locally on your device. This information is not sent to the developer. Removing the app removes this local data according to iOS behavior.

Network access

The game is designed to work offline and does not use network services during gameplay.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed version is released.

Support

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the public support page.
